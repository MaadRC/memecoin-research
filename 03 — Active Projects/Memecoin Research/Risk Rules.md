## Rules
1. **Fixed small size.** Same % every trade. Don't scale up because the last one won.
2. **Daily loss cap.** Hit it, close the app, done for the day.
3. **Take-profit plan.** Write it before entry. Example: at 2x, sell enough to recover the initial stake. Then the rest is free money to manage. Set your own levels: `____`
4. **No averaging down.** A falling price isn't new information in your favor.
5. **No trading when tilted.** After a loss streak, fatigue, anger or FOMO: stop. Pre-set a break length: `____`
6. **No chasing.** Missing a move is fine. There is always another.
7. **Screen first.** No `Screening Checklist.md` pass, no trade.
8. **Keep trading money separate** from money you need (rent, bills, savings).
9. Every trade goes in the journal, including skipped ones you almost took.
